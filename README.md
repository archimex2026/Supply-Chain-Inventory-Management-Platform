## Project Overview

The Supply Chain & Inventory Management Platform is an end-to-end Business Analysis and System Analysis portfolio project focused on the analysis, design and documentation of a centralized solution supporting procurement, inventory management, warehouse operations, shipment management and operational reporting.

This project simulates a real-world initiative conducted within a medium-sized distribution company and demonstrates industry-standard Business Analysis, Requirements Engineering, Process Modeling, Data Modeling and Object-Oriented Analysis & Design (OOAD) practices.

The project focuses on business and system analysis activities rather than software development and implementation.

---

# Business Problem

Many distribution companies face challenges caused by fragmented systems, spreadsheet-based inventory tracking and manual procurement processes.

Common operational issues include:

- Inventory discrepancies
- Poor inventory visibility
- Inefficient procurement workflows
- Delayed order fulfillment
- Limited shipment traceability
- Weak supplier performance monitoring
- Manual reporting processes
- Lack of operational transparency
- Increased operational risk
- Difficult auditability

The purpose of this project is to analyze these challenges and design a complete solution from a Business Analyst and System Analyst perspective.

---

# Project Vision

To design a scalable and business-driven Supply Chain & Inventory Management Platform that enables efficient procurement, accurate inventory control, optimized warehouse operations and transparent shipment management while supporting data-driven decision making.

---

# Mission Statement

Provide a structured analysis and design framework for a modern supply chain platform through the application of Business Analysis, BPMN, UML, OOAD and Requirements Engineering methodologies.
