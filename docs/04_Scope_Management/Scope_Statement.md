# Scope Statement

## 1. Project Justification
To remain competitive and meet growing demand, LogiFlow SRL must abandon its legacy spreadsheet-based inventory tracking and fragmented procurement processes in favor of a unified Supply Chain & Inventory Management Platform.

## 2. Product Description
A web-based internal application tailored for LogiFlow SRL employees (Procurement, Warehouse, and Management) to track inventory in real-time, automate reordering, manage supplier relationships, and monitor outbound shipments.

## 3. Solution Boundaries (In-Scope vs. Out-of-Scope)

### In-Scope (Phase 1)
- **Supplier Module:** Registration, Evaluation rating calculation.
- **Procurement Module:** Automated Purchase Requests (PR) based on Reorder Point (ROP), Purchase Order (PO) creation, Management approval workflows.
- **Inventory Module:** Goods receipt, Stock allocation, Quarantine management, Cycle counting.
- **Warehouse Module:** Bin and Aisle management, Directed put-away logic.
- **Shipment Module:** Shipment creation, Carrier assignment, Tracking status updates.
- **Reporting:** Operational KPI dashboards.

### Out-of-Scope (Deferred to Phase 2 or entirely excluded)
- **Financial Accounting:** General Ledger integration, invoicing, tax calculation.
- **Human Resources:** Payroll, employee time tracking.
- **E-commerce Frontend:** Customer-facing storefront.
- **Manufacturing/Production:** Bill of Materials (BOM) management.

## 4. Key Deliverables
- **Business Analysis:** BRD, SRS, Stakeholder Register, CBA, RACI Matrix.
- **System Design:** UML Use Case, Class, Sequence, State, and Activity Diagrams.
- **Process Models:** BPMN TO-BE workflows.
- **UI/UX:** Wireframes for core screens.

## 5. Acceptance Criteria
The project will be considered successful if:
1. All core modules listed in the In-Scope section are designed and documented.
2. The RTM demonstrates 100% coverage between Business Requirements and Functional Requirements.
3. Key stakeholders formally sign off on the SRS and system architecture.
