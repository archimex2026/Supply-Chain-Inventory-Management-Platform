# Vision Statement

## The Vision
To design a scalable, intuitive, and data-driven Supply Chain & Inventory Management Platform that empowers LogiFlow SRL to achieve operational excellence. 

## Core Pillars
1. **Automation:** Reduce manual data entry and email-based approval routing.
2. **Visibility:** Provide a single source of truth for all inventory levels and locations.
3. **Traceability:** Track the lifecycle of an item from the moment a PR is created until it reaches the final customer.
