# Business Glossary

This document provides a standard definition for the terminology used across the Supply Chain & Inventory Management Platform to ensure alignment between business and technical stakeholders.

| Term | Definition | Synonyms |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Purchase Request (PR)** | An internal document generated by a user or the system requesting the purchase of goods to replenish inventory. | Purchase Requisition |
| **Purchase Order (PO)** | A legally binding document sent to a supplier, indicating types, quantities, and agreed prices for products. | PO, Order |
| **Goods Receipt (GR)** | The physical and systemic acknowledgment that ordered goods have been received at the warehouse. | Receiving, Intake |
| **Quarantined Stock** | Inventory that has been received but is temporarily blocked from being allocated to orders (e.g., pending quality inspection or damaged). | Blocked Stock, Held Stock |
| **Reorder Point (ROP)** | The inventory level at which a new purchase request must be generated to avoid a stockout. | Minimum Stock Level |
| **Allocation** | Reserving a specific quantity of an inventory item for a pending outbound shipment. | Reservation |
| **Cycle Counting** | An inventory auditing procedure where a small subset of inventory is counted on a specific day. | Stocktake, Inventory Audit |
| **SKU** | Stock Keeping Unit; a unique identifier for each distinct product and service that can be purchased. | Product Code |
