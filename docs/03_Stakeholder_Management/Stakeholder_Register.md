# Stakeholder Register

## Overview
This document identifies all key stakeholders for the Supply Chain & Inventory Management Platform, their roles, influence, and expectations. It is a critical artifact for communication planning.

## Internal Stakeholders

| Name / Role | Department | Interest | Influence | Key Expectations |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Logistics Director** | Executive Management | High | High | Increased visibility across the supply chain, ROI realization, scalable solution. |
| **Procurement Officer** | Procurement | High | Medium | Automated PO workflows, easy supplier evaluation, low-stock alerts. |
| **Warehouse Manager** | Warehouse Operations | High | High | Accurate inventory data, mobile-friendly cycle counting, efficient location management. |
| **Warehouse Operator**| Warehouse Operations | High | Low | Simple interface for Goods Receipts, clear picking lists, minimal manual data entry. |
| **Inventory Analyst** | Inventory Control | High | Medium | Real-time tracking, accurate data for forecasting, automated reports. |
| **System Administrator**| IT | Medium | High | Easy user provisioning, secure architecture, high availability. |

## External Stakeholders

| Name / Role | Organization | Interest | Influence | Key Expectations |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Suppliers** | External Vendors | Medium | Low | Clear and accurate Purchase Orders, prompt notifications on goods received/rejected. |
| **Customers** | End Consumers/B2B | Medium | Medium | On-time delivery, accurate shipment tracking information. |
