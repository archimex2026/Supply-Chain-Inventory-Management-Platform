# Stakeholder Profiles

This document details the specific profiles, pain points, and goals of key stakeholder groups interacting with the platform.

## 1. Profile: Procurement Officer
**Role description:** Responsible for sourcing goods, negotiating with suppliers, and ensuring inventory levels meet demand without overstocking.
**Current Pain Points (AS-IS):**
- Manually checks spreadsheets to find out what needs to be ordered.
- Approvals for large orders get lost in email threads.
- Difficult to track supplier performance over time.
**Platform Goals (TO-BE):**
- System auto-generates draft Purchase Requests (PR).
- Clear dashboard of pending approvals.
- Supplier scorecard auto-calculated.

## 2. Profile: Warehouse Operator
**Role description:** Responsible for physically receiving goods, putting them away, picking for outbound shipments, and cycle counting.
**Current Pain Points (AS-IS):**
- Paper-based receiving logs lead to data entry errors.
- Time wasted searching for empty bins.
- No real-time indication if an item is damaged or quarantined.
**Platform Goals (TO-BE):**
- Mobile or tablet interface for instant Goods Receipt (GR).
- Directed put-away logic (system suggests bin).
- Ability to flag items as quarantined instantly.

## 3. Profile: Logistics Manager
**Role description:** Oversees both warehouse operations and transportation.
**Current Pain Points (AS-IS):**
- Cannot easily see bottlenecks in fulfillment.
- SLA penalties due to untracked late shipments.
**Platform Goals (TO-BE):**
- Unified dashboard for all warehouse and shipment KPIs.
- Automated alerts for delayed shipments.
