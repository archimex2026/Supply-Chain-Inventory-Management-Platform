# Portfolio Case Study: LogiFlow Supply Chain Platform

## 1. Context & Role
I served as the Lead Business Analyst and System Analyst for LogiFlow SRL, a mid-sized logistics company struggling with fragmented data, manual PO approvals, and untracked inventory shrinkage.

## 2. Approach
I utilized a hybrid Agile/BABOK approach:
- **Elicitation:** Conducted stakeholder interviews resulting in the Stakeholder Register and Communication Plan.
- **Analysis:** Developed the AS-IS vs TO-BE Gap Analysis and defined clear Business Rules for procurement thresholds.
- **System Design:** Applied OOAD principles to create a robust Domain Model, ERD, and UML behavioral diagrams (Sequence, State, Activity) mapping out the automation logic.
- **Requirements Engineering:** Authored the BRD and SRS, directly tracing them using an RTM to ensure 100% coverage.

## 3. Key Challenges & Solutions
**Challenge:** Resistance from management regarding the automated approval workflow.
**Solution:** I designed a BPMN TO-BE model that clearly showed a $10,000 threshold rule. This visual representation assured management that high-value orders would still require their explicit approval, leading to immediate buy-in.

## 4. Value Delivered
The portfolio demonstrates my capability to take a complex, ambiguous business problem and translate it into a structured, developer-ready architectural and requirements package.
