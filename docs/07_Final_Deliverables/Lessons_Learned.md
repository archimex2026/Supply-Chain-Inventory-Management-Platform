# Lessons Learned

## 1. What Went Well
- **UML & BPMN Integration:** Using PlantUML to map out both business processes and system architecture proved highly efficient. It bridged the gap between business stakeholders (who understood the BPMN flows) and technical teams (who needed Sequence and Class diagrams).
- **Requirements Traceability:** Setting up the RTM early in the Requirements Engineering phase prevented scope creep during the functional specification mapping.

## 2. Areas for Improvement
- **Data Dictionary Timing:** The Data Dictionary should have been drafted concurrently with the Domain Model. Waiting until the SRS was completed caused a slight delay in finalizing the ERD.
- **Early Wireframing:** Stakeholders had difficulty visualizing the "Pending Approval" dashboard. Creating wireframes earlier in the elicitation phase would have accelerated the approval of the Business Rules.

## 3. Action Items for Future Projects
- Implement a strict naming convention for all UML artifacts from Day 1.
- Include a UX/UI representative during the initial AS-IS analysis to better understand user pain points in the physical warehouse environment.
