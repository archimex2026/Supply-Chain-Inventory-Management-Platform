# Project Roadmap: LogiFlow Platform

This roadmap provides a high-level timeline for the analysis, design, and eventual implementation of the Supply Chain & Inventory Management Platform.

## Phase A: Discovery & Initiation (Months 1)
- **Milestone 1:** Project Kickoff & Stakeholder Identification.
- **Milestone 2:** Finalization of Project Charter & Vision Statement.
- **Milestone 3:** Sign-off on Cost-Benefit Analysis (CBA) and Scope Statement.

## Phase B: Business & Requirements Analysis (Month 2 - 3)
- **Milestone 4:** AS-IS Process Mapping (BPMN) completed.
- **Milestone 5:** Elicitation workshops concluded; BRD (Business Requirements Document) finalized.
- **Milestone 6:** SRS (Software Requirements Specification) drafted and functional requirements mapped.

## Phase C: System & Solution Design (Month 4 - 5)
- **Milestone 7:** TO-BE Process Models (BPMN) approved by Logistics Director.
- **Milestone 8:** UML Architecture finalized (Domain Model, Sequence Diagrams, State Machines).
- **Milestone 9:** UI/UX Wireframes for Dashboard, Mobile Warehouse App, and Procurement modules validated with end-users.

## Phase D: Development & Integration (Month 6 - 9)
- *Note: This phase transitions from the Business Analysis team to the Software Engineering team.*
- **Sprint 1-4:** Core Database setup, Supplier & Procurement Modules.
- **Sprint 5-8:** Inventory Ledger, Warehouse put-away logic, Carrier API integration.

## Phase E: Testing, UAT & Go-Live (Month 10 - 12)
- **Milestone 10:** Requirements Traceability Matrix (RTM) validated against Test Cases.
- **Milestone 11:** User Acceptance Testing (UAT) conducted with Warehouse and Procurement staff.
- **Milestone 12:** Production Go-Live and transition to Operations & Maintenance.
