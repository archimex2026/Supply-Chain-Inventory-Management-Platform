# Project Charter: Supply Chain & Inventory Management Platform

## 1. Project Overview
The Supply Chain & Inventory Management Platform aims to centralize and optimize procurement, inventory control, warehouse operations, and shipment management for LogiFlow SRL. It transitions the company from fragmented, spreadsheet-based tracking to a scalable, automated solution.

## 2. Problem Statement
LogiFlow SRL currently experiences:
- High rates of inventory discrepancies and poor stock visibility.
- Inefficient, manual procurement and slow approval workflows.
- Suboptimal warehouse utilization and tracking.
- Limited shipment traceability resulting in delayed fulfillment.

## 3. Project Vision
To deliver a scalable, business-driven platform that provides real-time visibility and control across the entire supply chain, enabling data-driven decision making and operational excellence.

## 4. Objectives & Success Criteria
| Objective | Success Criteria |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Improve inventory accuracy** | 99% accuracy in physical vs. system stock within 6 months of launch. |
| **Accelerate procurement** | Reduce purchase order processing time by 40%. |
| **Enhance visibility** | 100% of shipments trackable in real-time by internal stakeholders. |
| **Process standardization** | Implementation of standard BPMN 2.0 TO-BE workflows across departments. |

## 5. Scope Statement
### In Scope
- Supplier Registration & Evaluation
- Purchase Request & Order Management
- Inventory Allocation, Transfer & Adjustment
- Warehouse Storage & Picking Operations
- Shipment Planning & Tracking
- KPI Dashboards for Supply Chain Metrics

### Out of Scope
- Financial Accounting / ERP Integration (Phase 2)
- Human Resources / Payroll
- Manufacturing / Production
