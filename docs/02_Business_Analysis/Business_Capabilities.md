# Business Capabilities

To support the TO-BE state, LogiFlow SRL must develop the following capabilities:
1. **Automated Reordering:** Systemic capability to calculate Reorder Points and trigger PRs.
2. **Dynamic Bin Management:** Algorithmic routing for picking and put-away tasks.
3. **Real-time Supplier Scoring:** Automated performance tracking based on OTD (On-Time Delivery).
